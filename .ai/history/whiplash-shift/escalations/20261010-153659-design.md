**Recommendation:** go ahead, with two changes: derive the wheel offset and scale from a single visual-radius constant in a new candidate subclass, and apply the rake as a `VisualRoot` transform instead of baking it into vertices. I did not run anything or open the vehicle catalog; this is from reading the three scripts.

**Approach (all in a new `tests/blackjack_blueprint_preview_vehicle.gd`, no edits to `vehicle.gd` or `wheel.gd`)**
- **Wheel size and offset:** hold `VISUAL_RADIUS := {"A": r, "B": r}` in the candidate. Override `_wheel_visual_config` to add `assembly_offset.y = visual_r - tuning.wheel_radius(end)`, and set `ModularWheelVisual.scale = visual_r / wheel.radius` in `_install_tire`. That resizes tire, rim, disc and caliper in one step; `_place_visual` only writes position and rotation, so the scale persists.
- **Rake:** override `_rebuild_visuals` to call super, then set `_visual_root.rotation.x` and `position.y` from a measured constant. The mesh stays level as traced, so overlays compare directly, and sockets rotate with it because they are children of `_visual_root` (`vehicle.gd:500`).

**Risks**
1. **Axle ignores the offset.** `_update_axle_visuals` (`vehicle.gd:479-480`) computes height from `ride_distance - radius` only. Override it: call super, then set each axle's y from the pair's `ModularWheelVisual.position.y`.
2. **Bogie double-correction.** Bogie wheels never cast, so their assembly sits at `offset.y` inside `_bogie_root`. Line 76 of the refined rig (`+ tuning.wheel_radius("B")`) is therefore already right once the offset is applied; changing it to the visual radius would sink the bogie. The bogie `Shaft` sits at root y=0 and needs `position.y = offset.y`.
3. **Stale placement.** `set_visual_scene` resets `_visual_offset` but never calls `_place_visual` (`wheel.gd:59-88`). After `apply_customization` with no physics tick (garage, screenshots), wheels sit at the wrong height. Call `wheel._place_visual(wheel.ride_distance)` after refresh.
4. **Style switch drops the offset.** `assembly_offset_a/_b` is read only from the selected wheel style (`vehicle.gd:571-578`), not top-level config. Putting it in catalog data would let a wheel-style change float or sink the wheels; this is why I derive it in code.
5. **Body no longer matches collision.** Collision boxes stay in the raked body frame, so the level silhouette diverges by about half-length × sin(rake) at the nose and tail. Expect visible clip or hover on contact; document it and leave the boxes alone.
6. **Rake is tuning-dependent and static-only.** It shifts with mass multiplier, spring rates, rest lengths and mounts, and with dynamic pitch and End A downforce.
7. **End A arch clearance.** Its visual tire is now larger than physical, so the top rises by twice the radius difference; check at full bump.

**Main tradeoff:** the node-transform rake leaves the GLB unusable in production without the candidate's transform. At acceptance it must move into `visual.rotation_degrees`/`offset` (`vehicle.gd:117-118`) with socket positions re-expressed, or be baked then.

**Alternative worth considering:** bake the rake into the mesh as planned. That gives a self-contained asset, but every tuning change means a re-export, and the source is non-level for top and front overlays.

**Smallest test boundary**
- **Physics parity:** run stock `Vehicle` and the candidate on the same spec and scripted inputs, sequentially. Assert transform, velocities and per-wheel `radius`, `position`, `ride_distance`, `compression` and `normal_load` match within 1e-5.
- **Contact:** when settled, every displayed wheel including the bogie has `assembly.global_y - visual_r` equal to contact y within 5 mm.
- **Axle and shaft:** each axle and the bogie shaft sit at the mean displayed centre of their pair.
- **Rake guard:** measured settled pitch equals the authored constant within 0.1°, so a tuning change fails loudly.
- **No-tick refresh:** offsets still hold after `apply_customization` with no physics step.
- **Scope gate:** `vehicle.gd` and `wheel.gd` are already dirty in the tree, so compare their hashes before and after rather than using `git diff`.
