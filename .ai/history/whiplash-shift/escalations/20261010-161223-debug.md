**Verdict:** most likely float32 noise, not a visual-side physics mutation. I read the code and have not run any of the checks below, so the checks are what confirm it.

**Evidence against a physics mutation**
- The candidate overrides no physics method. `blackjack_blueprint_preview_vehicle.gd` and `blackjack_refined_preview_vehicle.gd` override only `_build_wheels`, `_refresh_wheel_visuals_and_mounts`, `_build_visual_bogie`, `_install_tire`, `_update_visual_bogie`, `_update_axle_visuals` and `_process`. `Vehicle._integrate_forces` and `Wheel.cast` are inherited unchanged.
- The subclass touches only visual nodes: `wheel._place_visual` (`wheel.gd:218`) sets `_visual` only, and the axle and bogie code sets mesh heights and positions. `visual_wheels` also holds the two bogie `Wheel`s, but those are never in `wheels`, never cast, and have no collision.
- Mass, inertia, collision boxes, radius and hardpoints are asserted equal, and the body-motion and velocity checks passed over all 120 frames.
- The failures are sporadic: 67 of 480 wheel-frame comparisons (4 wheels × 120 frames). A real mutation would fail systematically, on the same wheels or from the same frame onward.

**Why the check itself is too tight**
- The ground is a box centred at x=0, so the two cars sit at x=-10 and x=0. That changes the float32 rounding of the ray origin, the hit position and the body transform. Float32 resolution is about 1e-6 m at 10 m, against about 1e-8 m near 0.
- `ride_distance = from.distance_to(contact_point)` and `compression = reach - ride_distance` (`wheel.gd:199-200`) inherit that noise.
- `vehicle.gd:747` computes `damper = compression_velocity(dt) * susp_damp`. That divides the compression delta by `dt` (about 17 ms at 60 Hz) and multiplies it by the damper rate. Then `load = spring + damper + arb`, where `arb` is `tuning.anti_roll * ΔCompression`.
- A 1e-6 m compression error therefore becomes roughly 0.01–0.5 N of load error, depending on `susp_damp` and `anti_roll`.
- The static load is about 3434 N, so one float32 ulp of the load itself is already about 2.4e-4 N.
- A 1e-3 N absolute tolerance is only about 3e-7 relative, which is 2–4 ulps. That is a bit-exact check disguised as a tolerance.
- The compression check at 1e-4 m is probably not the failing half. The label merges the two conditions, so the log can't tell them apart.
