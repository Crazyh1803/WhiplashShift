**Evidence of a deterministic stall loop, not random flakiness**
- KNIFEEDGE sits at idx 126–154 for the whole run, from t=30 to t=420.
- `swaps` is always `recoveries` or `recoveries+1` (11/10, 13/12, 26/26). Each cycle appears to go: `_recover()` → `_place()` at the checkpoint → drive ~25 points → hit a surface boundary where `_preferred()` changes → `swap_ends("cpu")` → `_turning` never finishes → `_recover()`.
- The loop recovers about every 15 s. That is too fast for `_stuck_time > 6` to be the trigger, because speed is 2–6 m/s, not below 1.
- That leaves two likely triggers in `cpu_driver.gd`:
  - **Turn timeout** (`_turn_time > 8.0`, line 83). `_turning` only clears when `forward.dot(route) > 0.8 and fwd_speed > -1.0` (line 81).
  - **Off-line timeout** (`_off_time > 3.0`, line 53). `off` is 3–4 m for most samples.
- Idx ~153 is also a choke point for Boilerfang. It crawls through at 2.8 m/s (t=180) and 6.8 m/s (t=330), so the trouble spot is in the course, not a Knifeedge-only glitch. Knifeedge fails to get through it.

2. **Off-line timeout.** The CPU counter-steers (`steering = -steering` while `fwd_speed < -1`) and drifts wide, so `off_line > width*0.65` for more than 3 s. Check: the same reason tag, and log `progress.off_line` against `width*0.65`.
