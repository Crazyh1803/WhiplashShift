# Roadside rendered acceptance: culling defect to fix before publication

Actual delegate Vulkan images fail visual acceptance despite127 headless checks and generic nonblank-frame QA passing. tower_a-q1-full/podium show only a featureless brick podium and almost-black coarse shaft; tower_b-q4-podium shows blank massing/garage proxy. barrel-4-free shows paving and a flat warm pool, with no barrel or flames. See assets/previews/city_roadside in delegate branch.

Diagnosis from renderer source: CityRoadside._batch groups distant placements of the same asset into ONE MultiMeshInstance3D and gives that NODE a short visibility_range. Godot visibility range is per GeometryInstance (merged MultiMesh AABB), not independently per contained transform. Thus full near towers disappear even when the camera is beside one and a coarse far tower remains; barrel/board batches similarly disappear, while CityRoadside.measure incorrectly assumes per-instance range selection. CityRoadsideFx.build groups all eight fires with one180m node range and suffers the same issue. Shared geometry may be reused, but range selection must be local to each placement or genuinely spatial chunks.

Requested correction: make finite-range roadside batches per placement (shared Mesh resource, one transform per MultiMesh) or small spatial chunks whose actual NODE bounds are used consistently. Set explicit correct custom_aabb so Dummy and Vulkan agree. Split fire effects the same way; preserve deterministic per-barrel phase and coal heights, safe bounds, allcounts and no extra lights. Measure actual per-node active geometry/draw surfaces and include all instances of any active batch rather than optimistic per-transform filtering. Recalibrate only from real measured numbers; keep original base320k/160 and explicit whole-street budgets. Add a regression with two widely separated transforms and an eye beside either: the near full-detail subject/normal barrel/effects must remain active and the far-LOD subject must switch using its own location, not their midpoint. Captured-pixel QA must verify subject geometry is active in addition to a nonblank world frame. Regenerate all roadside captures and inspect actual detailed fronts, barrels/flames/smoke and boards, not just recorded counts. No new models or texture generation is needed for this correction.
