# Human anatomy correction for the industrial market candidate

Initial inspected renders (vendor_front.png/vendor_face.png/customer_front.png) fail the agreed realism bar: fully exposed round white eyeballs, balloon-like head/painted features, skin bump scaled like coarse rock, detached trouser hems/ankles and shoes, and mannequin shoulder/limb transitions. The clothing/material work and shop can be retained where useful, but this human prototype must not be presented as an accepted realism benchmark.
