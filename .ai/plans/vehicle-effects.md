# Vehicle effects
Goal: readable tyre smoke, terrain viscera/chitlin spray, persistent skid marks, boost, damage smoke and swaps in Godot desktop.
Lead: integrate vehicle state (short boost earned by clean whiplash; Q input), cosmetic hit severity from accepted weapon hits, reset hooks and HUD feedback. Preserve physics tuning and existing terrain response.
Claude delegate: isolated VehicleEffects node plus integration-independent tests/QA scenes (new module, test files), reuse existing smoke/spark textures and code-native meshes; no new raster assets. Effects read contact/load/slip and active end; bounded world-space smoke/trails, ground-normal marks; signal-driven swap pulse and directional boost plume. Existing VisceraResponse continues terrain chunks.
Design review: decide minimal boost/damage state compatible with current race and item systems; no health/destruction system. Damage is cosmetic and decays/reset clears it. Boost changes acceleration briefly with cooldown and earned charge; no new dependencies.
Validation: ak-test import and regression suite plus new effects tests; rendered visual QA for both ends, skid, smoke, swap; independent ak-review, then commit and publish chat preview.

## Delivered and validated
- VehicleEffects owns bounded tyre/damage smoke, directional boost plumes, ten-second world-space skid strips and end-colored swap feedback; existing VisceraResponse remains the organic/chitlin spray owner.
- Q spends one charge earned by a >=70% retained whiplash; grounded loaded drive wheels and forward throttle are required. Boost stays within the friction-circle/top-speed model, cancels on braking/handbrake/swap, and resets on recovery/retry. CPU boost charges are intentionally unused by its existing speed planner.
- Accepted unshielded weapon hits create cosmetic damage smoke, which decays. Shield and protection hits create none. Player/CPU recovery clears effects and state.
- Claude implemented the module, tests and QA fixture, then corrected the independently found test defects. Claude design/debug escalation supplied the two-triangle ring correction after the torus exhausted the optional-supercharger budget.
- Independent review: Codex approved the effects module; its MED slope-normal finding was fixed and the Z-axis assertion added. Claude approved the gameplay integration and the subsequent visual fixes. Reviews retained per existing repository convention.
- Validation via ak-test in partitions: existing physics, terrain, items, attachments, racecraft, cues/camera/corner and all vehicle blueprint/refinement/consolidation scenes pass after the budget correction. Dedicated effects: 95 passed. Native OpenGL rendered QA: 16 passed, eight captures visually inspected. CPU: 73 passed across difficulty tiers and the flat control. Race: 37 passed, including a physical three-lap finish and retry.
- Optional-supercharger total with the new quad pulse: 39945 triangles, under the unchanged 40000 limit. Windows CPU coverage requires more than the old 360-second timeout; allowance increased to 900 for its four 420-second simulations and the long race scenario.
- Smoke silhouette uses an opt-in luminance/radial mask; existing combat-smoke materials retain their default. Screenshots are ignored local QA artifacts under .ai/captures/vehicle-effects.
