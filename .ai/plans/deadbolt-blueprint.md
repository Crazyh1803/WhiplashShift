# Deadbolt blueprint build

- Model the `80sFord.jpg` reversible truck/Mustang concept directly in Blender,
  preserving the contrasting square truck nose and low coupe nose.
- Author a reusable, idempotent source script and pack the original blueprint into
  the native `.blend` for later art-direction passes.
- Export a gameplay GLB and LODs, render one human-facing preview, and validate
  dimensions, materials, object count, and triangle counts with `ak-blender`.
