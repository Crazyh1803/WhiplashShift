# Visible terrain relief and shared contact motion

Goal: eliminate the disconnect between flat-looking boulders and vehicle movement; give viscera, gravel and water gentler distinct contact motion.
Lead: coordinate physics constraints, integrate changes, run numeric and driver-view checks, independently review and publish.
Claude escalation: recommend legible boulder forms whose visible and collision surfaces match, preserving safe high-speed motion; no random steering or globally retuned suspension.
Claude implementation: shared relief/material coordinates and faceted boulder geometry; generalized contact response and independent regression coverage, in scoped hand-offs. Code-native procedural geometry, no external asset files needed.
Validation: compare flat controls at both ends/speeds, keep support/drift/angle limits, prove visual heights and collider heights match, verify joins and stationary/airborne/reset behavior; render actual driver views before publishing.
Final rock safety contract: 16–20 cm crowns use broad angular paired footprints; at 50 m/s an axle may unload for at most 0.3 seconds while at least two wheels always support the chassis, no full airborne steps, roll below 5 degrees, yaw rate below 0.3 rad/s and lateral drift below 0.3 m. Soft-surface contact limits remain unchanged.
