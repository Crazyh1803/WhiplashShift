  and frontal landmarks in pixel coordinates with uniform scale within each view.
  Preserve the existing physical hardpoints/radii/forces; compensate static body

  editable high-detail source, semantic paint protection, UVs and a lower LOD.
- Verify candidate physics invariants, contact/seating, sockets, garage-style paint
  customization, topology and full regressions through ak-test. Run two-round-max
