# Season one and Block Circuit

## Authorized outcome
Plan three cups of three distinct races across City, Underworks, Foundry and Coldworks. Deliver a playable Block Circuit with a coherent biomechanical City environment, a finished representative stretch and reusable city scenery modules. Keep Split Shift available as a proving-ground reference. Publish tested chat branch to desktop preview; do not merge main.

## Split
- Codex lead: season design document and route diagrams; course selection/session integration; Windows-compatible test entry point; integration, visual QA, review fixes, publish.
- Claude design escalation (read-only): recommend minimal course selection boundary and safe new course geometry/scenery contracts.
- Claude delegate: isolated Block Circuit course authoring and deterministic code-native City scenery (asset lead for visual design), focused independent tests and capture harness. No bitmap assets or Blender models required. Own new files only; no main menu, existing course or physics changes.
- Independent review: ak-review after tests.

## Design constraints
Existing Bydo-Grid art direction remains authoritative: industrial towers, ceramic ribs, steel cavities, sinew, small cyan/amber lights; no clean neon metropolis. Road and braking cues must remain legible. City layout should contain a generous start/grid straight, flowing boulevard, tighter service district and a mixed-grip section rewarding end swaps. No new physics, elevated drivable roads, cup save formats or progression systems in this slice. Nine race design plans must state surface mix, route, landmark, challenge and progression.

## Acceptance
Block Circuit accessible in normal garage/race flow; Split Shift still selectable. Closed smooth sampled loop without intersecting road envelopes, valid checkpoints and start grid. Scenery outside all road envelopes with clear overhead corridor; no new decorative collisions. Three lap race/retry and CPU smoke coverage. Season roster 3x3, all four worlds represented with City 3 and others 2. Import, relevant regression tests and independent review pass; capture and inspect actual driver-height and overview images. Document which scenery is detailed vs blockout.
