# Recommendation: one data-driven street layer across all four quarters, built on `CityStreetBlock`

**Approach.** Extend `CityStreetBlock` so it reads a layout file. Today it hard-codes one ~276 m frontage. Instead it should read a per-quarter list of *frontage segments* from a new `assets/city/streets/route_layout.json`, place family GLBs through the existing `_place`/`_surface`/`_batch`/`reserved_bounds` path, and give each segment its own fallback. `CityScenery` (towers, skyline, crown) stays the base layer and only gives way where a segment actually placed something.

**Why.** The four guards the city needs already exist in this code:
- full-course `road_clearance` (`city_scenery.gd:39`), which loops over every segment, so it already catches other roads and curves;
- reservations that `_Kit._accept` respects (`city_scenery.gd:183`);
- MultiMesh batching;
- a deterministic `null` fallback.

So this is a generalisation, not an engine rewrite.

**Main tradeoff.** Segments are authored by hand, with about 40 numbers per quarter. That's more tuning work than procedural placement, but the result is deterministic, easy to review, and testable.

**Alternative worth considering.** Spline-swept frontage with fully procedural lots. It's more organic on curves, but it needs new mesh-sweep code and has a larger overlap risk. Defer it.

## Exact integration seams
| Seam | Change |
|---|---|
| `city_street_block.gd:6-22, 12-16` | Replace `SLOTS`/`START`/`END`/`ALLEYS`/`INFILL` with layout segments `{quarter, s0, s1, side, district, families[], alleys[], shop_scene}`. Keep `TRIANGLE_BUDGET` but make it per quarter. |
| `city_street_block.gd:84-98` | Remove the blanket `emission 0.12` fill. Remap materials by `resource_name` to a shared library: one `StandardMaterial3D` per id, cached in a static var, so batches share materials. |
| `city_street_block.gd:127-153` | Run the `populate` loop per segment. If a segment fails, record it in `rejected_segments` and add no reservation, so legacy scenery stays visible there. Only an empty layout or missing library returns `null`. |
| `city_street_block.gd:200-245` | Tile ground bands in pieces of ≤12 m, each at its own `s`. Today one straight box spans the whole length and will cut into the road on curves. Drop the 51-63 m rear lane/verge outside Q1. |
| `city_street_block.gd:295-311` | Build one MultiMeshInstance per family **per quarter**, plus a matching LOD1 instance (see below). |
| `city_street_block.gd:170-195` | Check solid overlap and road clearance against the oriented footprint polygon (4 corners, `Geometry2D.intersect_polygons`), not the AABB. A building rotated 45° has an AABB about 41% larger, which causes false rejections on diagonals. |
| `city_scenery.gd:236` | Delete the `emit_geometry` first-quarter hack. Reservations alone decide what gets suppressed. |
| `city_scenery.gd:214` | Street-dressing lights stay suppressed inside reserved segments. Count them against the global light budget. |
| `city_district.gd:83-97` | When the street layer exists, keep only `_place_landmark`. Its prop and building rows duplicate the new kit. |
| `industrial_market_benchmark.gd:119` | Make `_prepare_materials` static and reuse it for actor scenes. Don't instance the benchmark node in races, because it adds a vehicle. |

## Asset contract (one manifest, `city_families_manifest.json`, version 2)

**Eight families.** Most are conversions of existing models; two are new:
1. `habitation_workshop`
2. `market_habitat`
3. `service_tenement`
4. `freight_depot`
5. `redline_shop`: market/food hero. Decimate the ceramic (10.8k tris) to a LOD0 of ≤25k.
6. `row_workshop`: housing row, upgrading `shutter_workshop`
7. `utility_substation`: new, from transformer and cabinet parts
8. `transit_depot`: new, bus/taxi/fuel services

**Rules for every family GLB:**
- **Mesh:** one static mesh, as `_load_assets:73` requires. No lights and no collision.
- **Materials:** at most 10 surfaces, every material named with a library id. UV0 and COLOR0 on every surface, with COLOR0 holding multiply-wear.
- **Origin and size:** origin at front-centre on the setback line. Frontage is 12, 18 or 24 m (±0.05). Depth ≤22 m, height ≤18 m, which keeps the skyline legible.
- **Triangles:** LOD0 ≤22k each (average ≤18k). A separate LOD1 GLB has ≤2k triangles, ≤3 surfaces, and the same footprint within ±0.1 m.
- **Manifest entry:** footprint polygon, entrances, `front_setback_m`, up to 2 `light_anchors {pos, dir, type, kelvin, energy, range}`, and `sign_variants` (indices into the sign atlas).
- **Variants:** come from the material and atlas only, never from extra geometry.

**Shared PBR library.** At most 14 materials: concrete, block, 2 corrugated claddings, painted steel, structural steel, glass, roofing, awning, wood, ceramic, rubber, `sign_atlas`, and `lamp_glass`. Each has albedo, ORM and normal maps at ≤512²; the sign atlas is 1024². That makes ≤43 textures, VRAM-compressed to about 40 MB or less.

**Street kit.** Re-export the 8 street-kit pieces with library material names. Their geometry stays the same.

**Validator.** A new `tools/city_pipeline/validate_city_families.py`, modelled on `validate_industrial_market.py`.

## Budgets (measured, frozen)
- **Placed LOD0 triangles:** ≤240k per quarter, ≤900k city-wide (sum over instances). Unique mesh triangles in memory ≤450k.
- **Near-visible window:** sample the camera every 10 m along the route at driver height. LOD0 within 140 m plus LOD1 within 450 m must total ≤320k triangles. Active MultiMesh surfaces ≤160.
- **Actors:** vendor + customer pairs at no more than 2 shop scenes city-wide (≤4 figures, about 106k triangles). Plain instances with `visibility_range_end` = 60 m, counted separately at ≤110k in the window.
- **LOD switch:** LOD0 has `visibility_range_end` = 140 m with a 10 m fade. LOD1 has `begin` = 130 m and `end` = 450 m. The street kit ends at 180 m and the ground at 420 m.
- **Lights:** SpotLight3D street lamps about every 30 m, staggered, plus family anchors, with `distance_fade` beginning at 90 m over 30 m:
  - ≤110 light nodes city-wide;
  - ≤20 unfaded lights in any window;
  - ≤2 shadowed lights per window (shop scenes only).
- **Emission:** only on `lamp_glass` and `sign_atlas` surfaces, at most 1 emitting surface per family. A test asserts that every other surface has emission disabled.

## Old-scenery and overlap rules
- **Reservations:** `reserved_bounds` for a segment are the tiled ≤12 m footprints at depths 0-36 m, not one giant AABB. That keeps second-row towers (gap 38 m or more) and the whole skyline.
- **Curves:** segments are only authored where the heading changes by less than 10° over the segment. Corners get just the street kit and lamps.
- **Clearance:** every placed piece and ground tile must have `road_clearance` ≥3.5 m against the whole course.

## Measurable acceptance
1. **Per segment, in all 4 quarters:** zero `road_clearance` violations, zero solid-polygon overlaps, and ≥1 accepted segment per side.
2. **Determinism:** two builds produce identical `placements`.
3. **Fallback:** removing a family GLB or the library makes only the affected segments fall back, and everything else is unchanged. Deleting the layout file gives today's scenery, apart from the removed first-quarter hack.
4. **Skyline:** the skyline part count, and the count of towers with offset ≥38 m, are at least the pre-change baseline. Crown is present.
5. **Budget asserts:** every number above holds, plus the emission whitelist.
6. **Course data unchanged:** a hash of course points and vehicle data matches before and after.
7. **Night captures:** 8 driver-height and 4 sidewalk captures. Median facade luma is ≥0.08 within 25 m of a lamp, and <2% of pixels are clipped.
8. **Pipeline:** the validator passes on every real export. `ak-test` passes with the city, Block Circuit, benchmark and menu suites.

**Risks:**
- Clustered light cost rises on the integrated GPU, so profile one Q-window early.
- Removing the hack at `city_scenery.gd:236` changes the Q1 tower layout. Re-baseline it once and get explicit user sign-off.
