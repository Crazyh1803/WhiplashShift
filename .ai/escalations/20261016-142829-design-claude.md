## Recommendation: port Blackjack's station-traced cage to a new Deadbolt builder

**Approach.** Write a new `build_deadbolt_hardsurface.py` with two sibling modules, `deadbolt_trace.py` and `deadbolt_geometry.py`, on an `asset/deadbolt-hardsurface` worktree. Don't touch the current branch's staged garage work.

1. **Trace.** Hand-trace pixel stations from `references/80sFord.jpg` in the format of `blackjack_blueprint_trace.py:21`: sill, belt, roof, top half-width and greenhouse half-width at each station.
   - Get the scale only from the two wheel-centre pixels mapped to x = −1.72 and x = +1.66 (a 3.38 m wheelbase). The anchors fix the frame; never stretch the sheet.
   - Keep Deadbolt's Blender Z-up, Y-width frame. Blackjack is Y-up with Z as width and a `POSE` rake (`trace.py:13`), so **don't `exec` its geometry module**. It also builds its shell at module level (`geometry.py:200`).
2. **Shell.** Use a mirrored station cage with explicit support loops, after `blackjack_blueprint_geometry.py:134-195`:
   - 30–45 stations, denser at the cab/coupe transition near x ≈ −0.45, both noses and the arches.
   - Mirror modifier with clip, subsurf level 1 (render level 2).
   - `crease_edge` weights: 1.0 on the truck nose box, grille surround and roof rails; about 0.45 on the beltline and the coupe's hood shoulders.
   - Flare the half-width to about 1.02–1.05 at the arches; the current 0.95 leaves the 0.34 m truck tyre proud.
   - Cut genuine arches with EXACT Boolean cylinders (`geometry.py:197`) centred on the anchors, radius = wheel radius + 0.06. Then run the non-manifold weld pass from `build_blackjack_blueprint.py:150-165`.
3. **Greenhouse and glass.**
   - Truck: a near-upright windshield (rake about 20°) with a squared cab roof.
   - Coupe: a fastback windshield (rake about 60°) flowing into the low deck.
   - Model glass as inset panels, then ray-cast them onto the shell with `seat_glass` (`build_blackjack_blueprint.py:116`), rewritten for Z-up with clearance 0.0035.
   - Add seals and wipers, and keep them out of decimation (`is_windshield_trim`, line 50).
4. **Detail modules.**
   - Lamps: port `lamp_bezel` (`geometry.py:341`), using rectangular sealed beams on the truck and slim coupe units.
   - Brush guard: `rounded_guard_path` (line 558).
   - Seams and handles: `side_seam` and `door_handle`.
   - Also chrome bumpers and a recessed truck grille cut with `cut_box`.
5. **Wheels.** Build lathe profiles with `band` and a hand-connected vent plate (`blackjack_blueprint_wheels.py:6,17`): a 6-opening steel wheel with a beadlock ring for the truck, and a 10-hole turbine-style wheel for the coupe, plus tread.
6. **Paint and export.**
   - Assign the three paint channels per face on the one shell, as in `geometry.py:150-168`.
   - Export with `export_semantic_collection`.
   - Build LOD1/LOD2 with `preserve_paint_borders` so paint boundaries don't crack under decimation.
   - Check UVs with `smart_uv`/`uv_outlier_faces`, and check shape with `reference_plane`/`render_overlay`.

**Hard contracts with `godot/scripts/deadbolt_blueprint_vehicle.gd`:**
- **Material names.** Keep `Deadbolt_Paint`, `Deadbolt_Secondary` and `Deadbolt_Stripe`; the runtime matches `resource_name` at `deadbolt_blueprint_vehicle.gd:17-20`.
- **Wheel objects.** Every wheel part must be named `Wheel_Truck_*` or `Wheel_Coupe_*`, and its origin must sit at the wheel centre on the correct side of y. The runtime sorts parts by name prefix and origin sign (`deadbolt_blueprint_vehicle.gd:65-70`).
- **Node names.** Never put semantic names in node names ("Primary" contains "rim"; `build_blackjack_blueprint.py:235`).
- **Placement.** Ground stays at z = 0, given the catalog offset of −0.62 and rotation of 90°. Don't change the collider or any tuning.

**Validators.** Extend `validate_deadbolt_blueprint.py`. Its fixed values (95 meshes, 18–20k triangles, `validate_deadbolt_blueprint.py:29,53`) must be changed on purpose.
- The shell is closed, two-manifold and has positive volume.
- Wheel centres are within 1 mm of the anchors.
- The tyre-to-arch gap is at least 30 mm.
- Glass sits 0.5–6 mm off the shell, with no crossings (BVH check).
- Silhouette error is under 3 pixels against the traced stations.
- The shell mirrors within 0.05 mm.
- The whole rendered vehicle is 20–40k triangles, and LODs shrink monotonically.
- The paint material names are present.
- Godot: physics parity through settling, driving and end-swap, plus the existing tests in `consolidation_tests.gd`.
- Visuals: neutral side/top/front and three-quarter renders, checked with `ak-look`.

**Main tradeoff.** The traced, Boolean-cut cage gives a faithful result that Blackjack has already proven, but it costs the most authoring time, and Booleans on a subsurf cage need weld and repair passes.

**Alternative worth considering.** Build two separate cages, truck and coupe, joined at the reversible seam near x ≈ −0.4. Each end is easier to make faithful, and the drawing already shows a seam. The cost is a manifold join and paint continuity across it.

**Risks.**
- Material or wheel-name drift breaks the runtime silently.
- The current tests may hard-code Deadbolt's GLB path or mesh counts.
- Wider arch flares could clip the existing collider in the garage preview.
