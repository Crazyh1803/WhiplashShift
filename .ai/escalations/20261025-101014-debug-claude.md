## Diagnosis

**1. Gameplay obscuration hits the owner.** `race_items.gd:398-412` loops over every car, including the hazard's owner. Any car whose path comes within `radius` (5.0) of the hazard sets `obscured[i] = true`. There is no `i != owner` check. The owner drives straight through the trail they drop:
- `_drop` places the puff 3 m up and `body_length*0.5+4` behind the car (`:372-379`).
- `_tick_active` refreshes the tail while the car is within 3.0 m of the last drop (`:283`).

So the owner is permanently inside their own 5 m radius.

`obscured[0]` drives only the HUD "// SMOKE" text (`hud.gd:177`). The only other reader is `_smoke_blocks` (`race_items.gd:523`). It takes no owner argument, and `_target` calls it at `:434` and `:459`. So the owner's own plume also kills the owner's homing and aim lock.

**2. Camera clipping.** `camera_rig.gd:60-67` puts the camera 8 m+ directly behind the chassis. The hazard sits 4 m+ behind the car and 3 m up. Camera height is 4.2 and pitch is -13°, so the camera sits close to the puff's height. The 6 m puffs (`race_item_art.gd:274`, scaled ×0.85–1.03) therefore sit at, or just in front of, the camera plane. The cloud shader billboards and is unshaded, with `depth_draw_never`, so the puffs fill the screen.

The `CPUParticles3D` stream (`race_item_art.gd:316-356`) points the same way. It is emitted at `rear*(body_length*0.5+0.3)`, `local_coords=false`, with a velocity of 2–4 m/s over 0.8 s. It trails directly into the chase camera's line of sight, with `BILLBOARD_PARTICLES` quads scaled up to 1.5×1.5.

**3. Swapping ends.**
- The stream uses `-cars[i].forward_vector()` (`:362`), and `_fire_direction` is flat `forward_vector()` (`race_items.gd:533`). If `forward_vector()` follows the active end, the plume is dropped behind the new heading.
- The camera yaw sweeps toward the new heading with `forward_sign()`, so the camera swings round into the plume it just laid. That is the worst case for clipping.

## Minimal owner-safe patch

All three changes are rendering or gameplay-flag only. No physics changes.

**(a) Don't obscure the owner.** In `_tick_hazards`:
```diff
-				if hazard["id"] == "smoke":
-					obscured[i] = true
+				if hazard["id"] == "smoke":
+					if i != int(hazard["owner"]):
+						obscured[i] = true
```
Give `_smoke_blocks` an `except_owner := -1` parameter. Skip hazards where `int(hazard["owner"]) == except_owner`. Pass `index` at `:434` and the matching call at `:459`.

Keep the opponent effect as it is, so the check at `item_tests.gd:200` (car 1 inside smoke is obscured) still holds. Add one test: the owner inside their own smoke is not obscured.

**(b) Hide the owner's smoke from their camera without hiding it from opponents.** Use `VisualInstance3D.layers`:
- Put the smoke puffs and the smoke stream emitter on render layer 2 only. That means `root` children in `hazard()` and the smoke `CPUParticles3D` in `prepare_streams`.
- Per-owner hiding takes more than layers, since each camera needs its own mask. A simpler option fades puffs by proximity to the camera. Use `fade_distance` on the shader: add `uniform float near_fade`. Set `ALPHA *= smoothstep(near, near*2, -VERTEX.z)` in `fragment()` using view depth. Alternatively use `distance_fade_mode = DISTANCE_FADE_PIXEL_ALPHA` with `distance_fade_min_distance = 4.0` and `distance_fade_max_distance = 12.0` on the stream material.
- Prefer the shader/material near-fade. It handles the owner's own plume, and also stops any car's cloud blocking the camera when the player overtakes it. For the owner it is invisible, because the plume is behind the camera's focus anyway.

**(c) Reduce stream density and size for the owner.** Cap the stream's alpha at about 0.35 for `i == 0` with `color_ramp`, or cut `amount` for the owner. This is a rendering-only change. It keeps the stream as a readable "I am dropping smoke" cue without covering the screen.
