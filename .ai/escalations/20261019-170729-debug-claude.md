**Root cause: the car is planner-limited at corners, and swaps add the biggest chunk of avoidable time. The tires are not the bottleneck, and tightening the planner further will not reach +20%.** I did not run the sim, so the time budget below is an estimate from the track geometry and the sampled log.

**Tire limit vs swaps**

- **The tire limit is not conservative.** Both axles must carry equal lateral force at equal load, so the real cornering limit is `min(mu_A, mu_B)·g`, not the average.
  - On mud that is min(0.72, 1.43)·0.40 ≈ 0.29, against the planner's 0.43·0.65 ≈ 0.28.
  - On tarmac it is 0.86 against 0.73.
  - On gravel it is 0.58 against 0.455.
  - The 0.65 reserve is already roughly the physical limit, and the weak axle is always the rear, which is why an optimistic limit spins the car (`vehicle.gd:766`). Raising `corner_skill` or the reserve buys about 5% at best.
- **The braking confidence is conservative.** Mud decel is capped at `mu_comb·g·0.55` ≈ 2.3 m/s² (`cpu_driver.gd:191,216`), where about 4.2 is physically available. That roughly doubles every braking distance.
- **Swaps are the largest single item.** The B-zone physical terminal speeds are ~18.6 m/s on water, ~25 on mud and ~28 on boulders (drive vs `ROLL` resistance), so the 1,600 m of B terrain cannot beat ~64 s.
  - My estimate for the 147 s lap is about 40 s on tarmac, ~85 s on B terrain and about 20 s lost to the two swaps.
  - The swap triggers only when the current point's surface wants the other end (`cpu_driver.gd:92-93`). The 180° spin therefore happens inside the corner or on low-grip surface, after braking to 12 m/s.
  - The B→A swap also cuts speed from ~30 to 12 on a 190 m gravel straight.
- **The old planner got 15215 m with higher corner speeds.** Its mud cap was ~23 m/s against 16 now, yet it matched the new distance. That points to swap and spin time, and to recoveries, as the shared limit.

**Changes, in priority order**

1. **Swap early, on the straight.** Trigger the swap ~20 m before the surface boundary and cap speed at that trigger point, not at the boundary. The rotation then finishes on rumble or tarmac instead of in the mud arc.
2. **Raise the swap entry speed.** Set it to ~16 for hard, since the mud arc caps at 16 anyway. For B→A, consider 18–20 on the gravel straight.
3. **Raise hard `braking` from 0.55 to ~0.75.** Hold it there if recoveries appear.
4. **Make the spin direction follow the road.** `_turn_sign` is always +1.0 (`cpu_driver.gd:98`), so every swap spins the same way regardless of the next bend.
   - Use `signf(-dir_now.cross(dir_30m_ahead).y)` as a starting guess, and check it in sim because the reversing logic flips the steering.
5. **Use a racing line in arcs** (`cpu_racecraft.gd`, which I did not read). Run outside-inside-outside within the ~10 m of lane room. I'd expect around 10% on corner speed, but that is unmeasured.

I estimate changes 1–3 at about 12–15 s per lap, and change 5 at about 5 s. That lands near 15–18%, so +20% needs all of them.
