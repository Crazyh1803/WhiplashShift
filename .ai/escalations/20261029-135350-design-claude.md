**Recommendation: add a gated `CityStreetBlock` stage between the legacy kit and `CityDistrict`. The block publishes its reserved footprints explicitly, and the district consumes them.**

In `CityScenery.build` (`city_scenery.gd:25-28`), run: `_Kit.populate` → `CityStreetBlock.build(root, course)` (only when `use_city_kit` and `CityStreetBlock.is_available()`) → `CityDistrict.build(root, course, reserved)`. The review scene should call this same `CityScenery.build` instead of the custom placer in `city_neighborhood_visual_qa.gd:80-162`, so captures match production.

**Integration points to fix**
1. **Street walls collide with the paving.** `_street_dressing` places walls and posts at rail +6.5–7.1 m from −100 to 350 m, and they are still emitted in the quarter (`city_scenery.gd:188-205`, `_accept(parts, false)` emits by default). That puts them in the middle of the sidewalk, alley mouths and ramps. When the block is active, skip stations with s in 6..282. That also drops the three dressing lights at about 60/140/220 m, which pays for some of the ≤8 new lamps.
2. **The district would fill the block.** Props at setback 9 m every 14/26 m and buildings at setback 12–16 m (`city_district.gd:76-86`) land on the paving. Pass the block's flat AABBs (front strips, aprons, alleys, rear lanes) and add them to `obstacles` before `populate`. Leave the 46 m second row (`:87-90`) alone; it is the backdrop behind the block. Check that rear lanes end before about rail +45 m.
3. **Relay landmark.** All three relay slots (145/150/175, `:73`) are inside the block. The landmark would fail and `push_error`, which breaks tests. Move the slots past 282 m on the side opposite `exchange_hall` (+1 at 345). Confirm `quarter_length` and that nothing collides with `pump_citadel`.
4. **Don't let reservation happen by accident.** `_collect_obstacles` (`:158-172`) adds every `MeshInstance3D` under the scenery root as an obstacle. It reads MultiMesh bounds from `city_local_aabb` and falls back to `UNIT_BOX` when that meta is missing. If block nodes are parented under `CityScenery`, one wide ground mesh becomes a huge obstacle, and furniture batches without `city_local_aabb` get unit-box footprints. Either parent the block outside the scanned root, or set the meta correctly and rely only on the explicit reserved list.

**Clearance and surfaces**
- Run every module through `CityScenery.road_clearance` ≥ 3.5 m, as the existing code does. Start ground strips at exactly rail +3.5 m.
- The road ribbon sits at y = 0.02 (`track_builder.gd:231`) over a ground plane. Put the base strip at about y 0.04 or higher, paving top at +0.18, and stagger patches and gutters by at least 0.01 m so no two layers are coplanar.
- Use `sample()["pos"].y` rather than assuming y = 0.

**Budget (≤190k)**
- The four buildings use 123,103, which leaves about 67k. Tiling `sidewalk_panel` (450 tris) every 6 m on both sides costs about 41k, and curbs add about 17k. That uses up the budget.
- Make long runs procedural quads with the world-scale seam shader. Use kit panels and curbs only at frontages, ramps and alley mouths.
- Count tris from the manifest during placement, in a fixed priority order (buildings, then curbs, then lamps, then furniture), and record rejections in meta the way `CityDistrict` does.

**Fallback and determinism**
- All-or-nothing on the four buildings. If any is missing, build no block, reserve nothing, and restore the street dressing. The district then behaves as before, apart from the relay move.
- If street-kit GLBs are missing, keep the procedural strips and skip those modules.
- The reverse course shares `geometry()`, so the layout holds.
- Store `city_transforms` meta, because headless MultiMesh reads return identity (`city_scenery.gd:429`).
- When applying `COLOR_0`, cache duplicated materials per source material. Never edit a shared resource.

**Skyline and culling**
- The skyline ring (640–960 m) and the crown are unaffected.
- Set `visibility_range_end` of about 250 m on the furniture batches only.
- Note that guidance beads already add an omni light every 18 m per side, so lamps should be unshadowed with distance fade.

**Main tradeoff:** an explicit reservation list couples the block and the district through one API, but it keeps the overlap rules exact and testable. The alternative is a blanket s-range exclusion inside `CityDistrict`. It is simpler, but it also removes second-row content, which goes against the plan.

**Test risk:** the lower piece-count assertion in the existing district tests will legitimately drop. Re-base it on near-population roles, and assert that all 14 legacy asset roles still appear in the rest of the quarter.
