**1. The ROI mapping is likely still wrong (most likely).**
- `project.godot:28-32` has a 1600x900 base, `canvas_items` stretch and a 1280x720 window override.
- `Camera3D.unproject_position` returns coordinates in the visible-rect space, which here is 1600x900 or 1280x720 depending on stretch handling. The `* get_visible_rect().size` scale in `city_roadside_qa.gd:267` can therefore be 0.8 or 1.0.
- In both runs the ROI missed small subjects. Large towers survive because their ROIs are huge. Boards, flames and some barrels are small and give exactly 0, which is what a misplaced small rectangle gives.
- Fix: stop using `unproject_position`. Project with the camera matrices into the captured image's own pixel grid.
