# Recorded contact layers (CC0)

These recordings are adapted from the Godot Engine demo projects, `3d/ragdoll_physics/sounds`:
https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects

- `contact_small.wav`: `impact_small.wav`, dorian.mastin (2017), CC0 1.0.
  https://freesound.org/people/dorian.mastin/sounds/381626/
- `contact_heavy.wav`: `impact_big.wav`, FFeller (2020), CC0 1.0.
  https://freesound.org/people/FFeller/sounds/532873/

Edited for mono 22.05 kHz PCM, equalization, gain limiting and tail fades.
Original license notices are retained alongside the files. ItemAudio layers these
recorded contacts with broadband cracks and filtered low-frequency noise; impact
and hit cues contain no musical oscillator tones.

Pickup feedback is an original synthesized arcade FM sweep with stereo delay taps.
It contains no recordings or samples from R-Type. Pickup playback uses a local stereo
voice; weapon impacts remain positional. Impacts combine a dry noise crack, the
heavy recorded contact body and a short irregular debris tail.
