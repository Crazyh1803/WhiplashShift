# Industrial supply crates, folding weapons and persistent oil

Supply crates use the Blender-authored `godot/assets/items/crate/item_supply_crate.glb`. Rebuild with `ak-blender .ai/blender/build_item_crate.py`; the candidate source and preview are in `assets/generated/crate/`. Numeric QA reports 1,636 triangles, six meshes/materials, zero text objects and no errors. The crate has armored rails, vents, seals, chevrons and amber/cyan status lamps. No world-space label, question mark or countdown remains. The segmented pad shows recharge; HUD collection confirmation and item odds are preserved.

The existing weapon GLBs sit in hinged deck hatches with lift arms. Deploy is 0.45 s, retract is 0.35 s. Old-end retraction finishes before the new end deploys. The APIs request a pose, advance time and query readiness separately. Firing waits for a fully deployed visible muzzle; one press is buffered without early ammunition use. Pending burst rounds pause during end changes. Last ammunition triggers retraction, while recovery/results/reset cancel queued fire and hide safely. Collider geometry and the original deployed muzzle locations are unchanged.

Oil patches are place-anchored and do not expire with a timer or the owner's position/availability. A same-road swept crossing consumes each patch once and applies the existing 1.2 s grip effect; a shield consumes it without grip loss. Route and height checks isolate crossing road sections, and teleport-sized sweeps do not count. The active dispenser does not consume its own oil; it can hit its own traps after dispensing ends. Consumed stationary tails can replenish while the power-up is active. Race reset/results clear all traps.

Persistent oil is deliberately excluded from transient smoke eviction. Arbitrarily evicting oil at a size cap would contradict the requested persistence; the standard smoke cap still applies. Oil does not send redundant opacity updates every frame. No save format or across-race persistence was introduced.

Validation: focused ak-test passes 194 item checks, 864 attachment checks across all seven vehicles, 88 deployment/oil checks and 12 camera checks. Rendered/audio QA passes 68 checks on Helix and Boilerfang, including the text-free crate, charge pad and a four-second deployment/fire/retract/end-swap sequence.
