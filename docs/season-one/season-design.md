# Season one: three cups, four worlds

Approved direction: nine distinct races, spread across three cups. City appears three times; Underworks, Foundry and Coldworks appear twice each. Only Block Circuit is implemented in this first slice. Other routes below are design targets, not playable courses. Cup scoring, unlocks and saved championship progress are later work.

## World kit and visual language

All four settings share the Bydo-Grid palette, chiseled industrial silhouettes, ceramic ribs, steel cavities and parasitic sinew. Keep the road brighter and simpler than the surroundings. Cyan and amber identify compact guidance fixtures; they do not flood the road with emission. Surface appearance must retain existing handling meanings.

## Block Circuit construction brief

Target a first-cup lap with two overtaking opportunities and one clearly taught surface change. Use generous constant-width lanes and rounded bends; avoid blind junctions, jumps, crossovers and decorative objects in the driving corridor. Keep the starting grid on a dry straight with room before the first brake point. Existing cars, physics, items, countdown, checkpoints and three-lap rules stay authoritative.

Scenery has three scales: nearby modular street walls and supports, medium-distance ribbed buildings, and a stepped skyline. The opening boulevard and first corner form the detailed sample; the remaining circuit gets coherent lighter dressing. A repeated kit should create a city district, with a dominant communications landmark providing orientation. Utility bridges may cross overhead only above a generous vehicle/camera clearance.

A finished representative stretch means a visually reviewed road edge, readable corner, near/mid/far silhouettes, restrained local lighting and no blocked route. It does not imply bespoke production models or that every metre of the city has final art. Add bespoke assets only after the layout survives driving tests.

## Build order after this slice

1. Drive and refine Block Circuit: corner rhythm, surface approach, overtaking, camera visibility and CPU consistency.
2. Complete its remaining scenery using the validated kit.
3. Build Drain Run and Slagline to complete Cup 1 before producing the later cups.
4. Add championship flow and scoring once three enjoyable races exist.
5. Produce Cups 2 and 3 using distinct layouts and shared world kits.
